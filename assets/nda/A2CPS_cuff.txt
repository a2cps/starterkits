
A2CPS REST (Resting State fMRI)

Two BOLD scans are acquired (6 min duration) while participants wear an inflated Hokanson Rapid Cuff (https://hokansonvascular.com/products/133448) on one leg. 

Pressure is constant throughout each scan. The first scan uses a subject specific inflation pressure, and the second scan uses a standard (120 mmHg) inflation pressure.
