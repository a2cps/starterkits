
A2CPS REST (Resting State fMRI)

Two BOLD scans are acquired (6 min duration) while participants wear an uninflated Hokanson Rapid Cuff (https://hokansonvascular.com/products/133448) on one leg.

Participants are instructed to keep eyes open and fixate on a cross.

One REST scan is done prior to cuff inflation for two A2CPS CUFF scans, if applicable, and the other after.
